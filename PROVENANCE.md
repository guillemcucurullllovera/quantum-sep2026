# Provenance and license boundary

This is the complete reviewed source union of the existing local `main` and
`quantum/quantum1b-research-20260930` branches, preferring the later research branch
on shared paths and checking the accepted hygiene checkpoint. SOURCE_COVERAGE.json
records source branches, commits, file hashes, destinations and exact exclusions.
Private source clones remain unchanged. No private Git objects are inherited and
no historical contribution timeline or author dates are synthesized.

The owner previously confirmed authority to redistribute/license first-party code
and explicitly directed this comprehensive parent export on 2026-11-02. The original
first-party proprietary license is replaced by Apache-2.0 under that authorization;
original attribution to Asymmetrica Investments and Francisco is not reassigned.
This records the owner's representation, not an independent ownership opinion.

Unlike the narrow standalone exports, this parent preserves the complete upstream
integration surface: permissively licensed vendor/adapted packages are retained
with their original headers, notices and LICENSES/ texts. They are classified as
THIRD_PARTY_OR_MIXED_PERMISSIVE, never claimed as first-party work or relicensed.
The existing THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md gives the upstream component mapping. Apache-2.0
and MIT permit this redistribution subject to the preserved attribution. Restricted
or copyleft materials, credentials, private records and non-redistributable data are
not authorized by the first-party grant and are not intentionally included.

Executed notebooks are reduced to code-only scripts under notebook_sources/;
their outputs/metadata are not redistributed. Internal operational/review prose,
historical result archives and retired investor presentations are excluded.
Scientific implementations remain intact even when private data is unavailable.

Release engineering, configuration sanitation, packaging, additional validation and
documentation are AI-assisted. See RELEASE_CHANGES.md for deviations from source.
